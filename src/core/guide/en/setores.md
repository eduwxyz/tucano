# Sectors (when the subject is another area's)
> Each team on the board is a sector with a head. How to name it, say what it solves and how a request passes from one sector to another.

Every team put together in **Team** is a **sector**. Whoever sits at its top (answers only to you and has someone below) is the **sector head**. Inside the sector nothing changes: each one asks those below and its colleagues.

## One way in (if you want it)
To talk to a single agent, put one at the top, make the heads answer to it and, on the board, click it and tick **Entry point**. It sits above the sectors, outside all of them: you ask it, and it passes each part to the right sector's head. The sectors become the teams below it. You can still talk to any head directly.

None of this is required: you can have only sectors, side by side, or only loose agents, each one talking to you.

## Giving it a name, and saying what it solves
On the **Team** board, click the sector's name (the coloured badge above the team). In the panel:
- **Sector name**: with no name, it shows "Sector of" and the head's name.
- **What this sector solves**: one sentence, for example "Bugs, errors in the system and integrations". The other heads go by it to know when to send something here.

It saves by itself when you leave the field. Only an admin changes it; the others see it.

## When the subject belongs to another sector
1. Whoever is in the team and sees that the subject is not the sector's passes it straight to the **sector head**, saying why, even with someone in between. What it only cannot do, it passes to its own head.
2. Only the **head** asks another sector, and always **to its head**. Never straight to someone in that team.
3. The head that receives it decides who in the sector does it, or answers saying why it is not its sector's.
4. The answer comes back the same way, to whoever started it.
5. If no sector takes care of the subject, the agent asks you.

## On the board
- Each sector sits on a coloured background, with its name and what it solves; the head carries the **Sector head** badge.
- A request between sectors is a dotted line between the two heads, with the sector's name.
- **Now** shows, for example, "Teo → Technical Sector".
- In the sector's panel, the **Requests from other sectors** appear.
- In the conversation, the request shows as **Passed to the head**, or **Asked** the sector.

## Limits
The usual ones (the Team guide): up to 5 requests per reply, 12 open and a maximum number of requests in a row on the same job. Nobody asks back whoever is already waiting on them.

## Exporting the team
**Export team** carries the name and what each sector solves.
