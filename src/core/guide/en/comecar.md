# Starting on Tucano
> What Tucano is, the introduction, the Tuca, the sidebar and the Settings tabs.

Tucano is a team of AI agents running on your computer. You talk to them in channels, as in a work chat. They search, write, code and use your tools, and you decide how far each one goes. Conversations, memory and logins stay on the computer: nothing goes through a Tucano server.

The agents run in the programs you already use (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi, Antigravity CLI, Cursor CLI, Grok or Cline CLI), the way you set each one up. See the **motores** page.

## The introduction
The first time, the app guides you through a few steps: your name; connecting the AI (whatever is already there appears tested; whoever has nothing chooses ChatGPT or Claude and Tucano installs the official program and opens the login in the browser, with no terminal); the Tuca's question about what to take off your back; and the first agent, which joins **#geral** with its first message already written, waiting for you to press send. API key, company gateway or another program: **I use an API key, company gateway or another program**. The models behind the scenes are picked on their own (**Change** changes them). To go through it again: **Settings › General** › **See the introduction again**.

After that, balloons explain the app while you use it; **Skip the tour** ends it. The sidebar holds the **Missions**, which tick themselves off. On a new installation the sidebar starts short and each mission unlocks a part (Connectors, Channels, Routines, Memory and Wiki); **Show everything at once** shows it all. Hidden, they come back in **Settings › General** › **Show the missions**.

Whoever joins a team by invitation sees a short welcome: who invited them, the agents, the name and the deal (what is said to one agent alone only that person reads; in channels, the whole team sees it).

## The Tuca
The Tuca sits at the top of the sidebar. It builds agents: you describe a task, it asks a few questions and proposes an agent in a card. Nothing is created without you clicking **Create** (or **Edit first**, or **Discard**).
Several changes asked together arrive as a group (**1 of 5**), each with **Apply**, plus **Apply all** and **Discard all**. The conversation is kept when you leave and come back; **New conversation** starts over.

## The sidebar
- **Approvals**, **Memory**, **Wiki**, **Routines**, **Skills** and **Report**.
- **Channels**, **Direct messages** and **Agents**. Archived agents sit under **Archived**, at the end of the list.
- At the bottom: **Usage and limits**, **Connectors** and **Settings**.
- At the top, the workspace's name (switches workspace) and the pause-everything button.
- ⌘K (Ctrl+K off the Mac) opens **Go to a channel, agent or page…**.

## Settings
- **General**: workspace name, what the agents call you, language, **Context** (what every agent reads before each reply), dictation, notifications, updates, team and backup, danger area.
- **Models**: the harnesses and the accounts, the **Router** and the **Memory** model.
- **Agents**: the agents' browser, routines (open on start, quiet hours) and limits.
- **Phone**: the Telegram bot.
