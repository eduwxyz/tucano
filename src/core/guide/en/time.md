# Team (several people in one Tucano)
> Using the same Tucano with other people: inviting, roles, what belongs to whom, For you, calling a person, who covers for me, routines, Activity, removing someone and the first invite.

A team only exists on Tucano on a server (yours or Cloud), because it has to stay on for everyone. The Tucano on a computer is one person's.

## Inviting people
In **Settings › People**, **Invite**: choose the role and what the person does ("finance"). The link is good for one use, for 7 days. The person opens it, gives their name and comes in, from the browser or the desktop app (Settings › Servers). Pending invites have **Revoke**.

## Roles
- **Owner**: everything, including changing admins.
- **Admin**: agents, connectors, skills, settings, inviting and removing members, approving any action.
- **Member**: chats, creates channels and routines for themselves, approves the actions of the agents they own and answers what is asked of them.

Whoever creates a channel or an agent owns it. Only the channel's owner and the admins change the channel's name, topic and agents.

## What belongs to whom
- **Direct messages** with an agent: only the person reads them, not even Tucano's owner.
- **Memory**: what is learned in a DM stays in that person's DMs; in channels only what was said in the channel goes in.
- **Screen preferences**: theme, sounds, sidebar order.

## For you
It gathers the approvals you decide and the questions the agents asked you. An approval goes to the agent's owner (and the admins), with the name of whoever asked.

## Calling a person
- **@name** notifies the person. If it is only for people, no agent answers.
- An agent can ask a person and wait for that task only. Only they, an admin or whoever covers for them answers. In a DM the agent asks your ok first, and the person sees the question alone.
- With no answer in 4 hours, whoever asked is told and can answer.

## Who covers for me
In **People**: pick someone and until when. In that period the person answers and approves on your behalf.

## Routines
**Only for me** notifies only you. **The channel's** belongs to the team, only an admin creates it and it notifies nobody; whoever wants it turns on **Let me know**.

## Activity
In **Settings › Activity** (owner and admins): who came in, left, approved and changed what. Never the content of DMs.

## Removing someone
Access ends at once. The person's DMs and memory stay for 30 days (they come back if the person is invited again) and are then deleted; the owner can delete them sooner. Their channels' agents and routines pass to the owner.

## The first invite
Until it, summaries and the wiki learn from your DMs. On the first invite, without deleting anything:
- the channels' summaries are redone without anything from the DMs;
- the messages of earlier channels and the wiki's old history stay with the owner only;
- pages that came from your DMs are kept until you **Release to the team**.

## Limits
The Claude or ChatGPT subscription is for one person: on a team, prefer an API key or the company gateway. Each agent's browser is one for the team: a login made in a DM stays open for the others. Connectors and limits apply to the whole Tucano.
