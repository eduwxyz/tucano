# Team (who answers to whom)
> An agent above the others hands work to those below, each in its own channel, and gets the answer back. How to set it up, what happens in a request, questions, cancelling, deadlines and limits.

By default the agents only talk in the same channel. When one **answers to** another, the one above hands work down and the answer comes back to where it was asked. You talk to one, and it divides the request.

## Setting it up
In **Team** (two or more agents). Each agent is a card on a board: drag the background to move, ⌘ and scroll (or pinch) to zoom. With nobody linked, **Who stays at the top?** makes the others answer to them.
- **Link**: pull a card's bottom dot to whoever answers to it, or its top dot to whoever it answers to (you: nobody).
- **Unlink**: click the line and the **×** (or Delete). Every change shows **Undo** (⌘Z).
- **Tidy up**: dragging a card moves only its place, with the team (⌥: only it); **Tidy up** restores the automatic drawing.
- **Ask**: the box at the bottom talks to the agent you clicked, or to the one at the top.

Clicking an agent shows channels, team and requests; **Now** lists the open ones.

Or on the agent's page, under **Channels and skills**: **Answers to**.
- An agent answers to one only. It asks everyone below it and its colleagues (whoever answers to the same agent); never upwards or another branch. A line has at most three agents.
- It does not apply to those below it, nor to the Tuca.
- Once an agent is archived, its team answers to whoever is above.

## What happens to a request
1. You ask the agent at the top.
2. It hands each one their part: each request becomes a thread in the channel of whoever does it (or their DM), in the asker's name.
3. Its reply shows **Asked …**, with **Open the thread**.
4. When the thread ends, the request's conversation gets a note (**answered**) with **See the answer**.
5. The asker replies once more when everything is back. A request stuck on an approval of yours does not hold up the others.

Where it stays: in the channel of the agent whose **Topic** covers the subject. What belongs to no channel, or an agent with no channel, goes to their DM with you. In doubt, the first channel.

## Questions
The doubt of whoever received it goes to whoever asked, not to you. They answer what they know; what is your decision they ask, on the usual card, and pass it on. You can answer in the thread too.

## Approvals
Each agent asks for its own, to you.

## Cancelling
Tell the asker to cancel: whoever was doing it stops, and so does what it asked further down. **Stop** on a thread's reply ends the request and tells the asker.

## When it does not finish
Failed, stopped at the limit or paused at the ceiling: the asker is told at once, with the reason. Past the deadline, or the app closed mid-way: once.

## Limits
Up to 5 requests per reply, 12 open per agent and 3 in a row on the same job. Nobody asks back whoever asked them. Paused, none goes out. Each agent spends from its own limit.

## Teams of people and the team file
With more people, a request only reaches channels whoever asked can see. **Export team** carries who answers to whom.
