# Team (who answers to whom)
> An agent above the others hands work to those below, each in its own channel, and gets the answer back. How to set it up, what happens in a request, questions, cancelling, deadlines and limits.

By default the agents only talk in the same channel. When one **answers to** another, the one above hands work down and the answer comes back to it. You talk to one, who divides the request.

## Setting it up
In **Team**, in the sidebar. Each agent is a card on a board: drag to move, ⌘ and scroll (or pinch) to zoom. With nobody linked, pick **Who stays at the top?**.
- **Link**: pull a card's bottom dot to who answers to it, or its top dot to who it answers to.
- **Unlink**: click the line and the **×** (or Delete). **Undo**: ⌘Z.
- **Tidy up**: dragging a card takes the team along (⌥: only it). **Tidy up** restores the automatic drawing.
- **Ask**: the box at the bottom talks to the agent you clicked, or to the top one.

Clicking an agent shows channels, team and requests; **Now** lists the open ones.
- It answers to one only. It asks those below it, its colleagues, its own head and the sector head; another sector, only through the heads.
- Not those below it, nor the Tuca. Once an agent is archived, its team answers to whoever is above.

## What happens to a request
1. You ask the agent at the top.
2. Each part becomes a thread in the doer's channel (or their DM), in the asker's name. The whole subject stays in it.
3. The asker does not write to you then. Under its reply: one line per request, **Asked …**, the channel, how it stands (**working**, **with a question**, **waiting for your approval**, **done**) and **Open the thread**.
4. When everything is back, it answers you once in your message's thread (**See the answer**). A request stuck on your approval does not hold up the others.
5. To go on with the subject, answer in that thread: the request follows in the doer's thread.

Write in a request's thread and the doer answers you there; that goes into the result.

Where it stays: the channel whose **Topic** covers the subject. What belongs to no channel, or an agent with no channel, goes to their DM with you. With more people, a request made in a DM stays in a DM unless you name the channel (**#engenharia**).

## Questions
The receiver's doubt goes to whoever asked. It answers what it knows without writing to you; a decision of yours it asks in your message's thread, and passes it on. Or answer in the request's thread.

## Approvals
Each agent asks for its own, to you. A request's approval also shows on its line: approve it there.

## Cancelling
Tell the asker to cancel: whoever was doing it stops, and so does what it passed down. **Stop** on a thread's reply ends the request.

## When it does not finish
Failed, stopped at the limit or paused at the ceiling: the asker is told at once. Past the deadline, or the app closed mid-way: once.

## Limits
Up to 5 requests per reply, 12 open per agent and 3 in a row on the same job.

## Teams of people and the team file
With more people, a request only reaches channels whoever asked can see. **Export team** carries who answers to whom.
